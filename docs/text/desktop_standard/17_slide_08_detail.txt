Structure & Authority
Four divisions: Academic Affairs (65), Digital Marketing & CRM (20), IT & OneHub ERP (12), Registry/Finance/HR (18) + 5 top managers.
Authority matches responsibility: the Marketing Director controls the ad budget and can reject unapproved agent invoices.
Delegation & Unity of Command
Delegate authority, never accountability: Registry Supervisors approve add/drop and exam clearance within 24 hours.
Unity of command: creators report only to the Social Media Lead.
Centralised: tuition and the 15% commission policy. Decentralised: CRM replies within 2 hours.
