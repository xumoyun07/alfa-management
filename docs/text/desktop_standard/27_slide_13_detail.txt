5-Area Matrix
Financial: total spend at most $94,500 ($236.25/student); cost per direct student $161.54.
Operations: CRM reply within 2 hrs; student requests within 24 hrs (vs. 5–7 days); paper -90%.
Quality: 5 Reels/week, 20K followers, student score at least 4.5/5.0.
HR: at least 95% of staff pass the OneHub test; 100% digital leave.
Strategic: direct intake 20% / 40% / 55% / 65% by quarter.
Variance Calculations
Variance = Actual − Standard.
Example: 240 direct students → 160 agents × $375 = $60,000 vs. $52,500 standard = +$7,500 unfavourable.
Example: Q2 direct intake of 30% vs. 40% standard = −10 percentage points → trigger the $150 scholarship review.
Corrective actions: freeze weak ads, audit agent invoices, reassign pending requests, 1-on-1 IT coaching.
