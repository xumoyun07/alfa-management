FIB 2074 Introduction to Management · ALFA University College

From 100% agent dependency to 65% direct enrolment and one paperless platform.

An integrated 12-month POLC turnaround restructuring recruitment channels and administrative speed across all 120 staff (assumption).

Problem 1 · Recruitment Drain 100% Agent Dependency

All 400 students currently arrive through 108 external agents at 15% ($375) commission, costing $150,000 annually. Shifting 260 students (65%) to direct social channels saves $97,500 gross.

Problem 2 · Paper Bottleneck Manual Approvals & Queues

Physical registration and fee slips take 5–7 days to process (our estimate). Deploying ALFA OneHub (CRM, ERP, HRM self-service, our proposal) delivers digital service in under 24 hours.

Verified Institutional Profile

  • Campus Location: Lot 13, Persiaran Kewajipan, USJ 1, 47600 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.
  • Programme Range: 68 full-time programmes across 7 Schools and 4 Centres (ALFA website).
  • History & Motto: Established 1998 as Alif Creative Academy; acquired 2017 by Nusantara Education Group; University College since 2021; motto “A Leader For All”.

Working Assumptions & Disclaimers

  • Staff Scale: 120 full-time staff across administrative and academic units (our assumption).
  • Paper Queues: 5–7 day turnaround for manual course registration, fee clearance, and exam slips (our estimate).
  • Social Media: Instagram baseline ~2,100 followers (our observation), scaling to 20,000 via TikTok and Reels.
  • Platform Architecture: ALFA OneHub unified cloud portal combining CRM, ERP, and HRM self-service (our proposal).
  • Commission Breakdown: 15% ($375/student) total commission split into 10% sub-agent ($250) + 5% master agent ($125) (our assumption).
