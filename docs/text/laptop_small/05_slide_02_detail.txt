Challenge 1: 100% Agent Dependency
400 of 400 students come through agents; 0 students from direct online channels.
12 university master agents × 8 consultancy sub-agents = 96 active sub-agents.
Commission per agent student: 10% ($250) sub-agent + 5% ($125, our assumption) master agent = 15% ($375).
Baseline: $100,000 + $50,000 = $150,000 / year; with $10,000 low-impact marketing (assumption) = $160,000 ($400.00 per student).
Challenge 2: Paper Bureaucracy
No unified CRM, ERP or HRM: (our observation) enrolled students wait about 5–7 days (our estimate) for several paper approvals for course registration, exam slips and letters.
Weak social media: only ~2.1K Instagram followers and a confusing duplicate account.
Result: slow service, paper waste and no smooth path for online leads.
