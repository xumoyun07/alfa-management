ALFA University College: Profile & Verified Facts

From Alif Creative Academy (1998) to a 68-programme University College generating $1,000,000 in annual first-year tuition.

Based in Subang Jaya under Nusantara Education Group (since 2017) with the motto “A Leader For All.” Annually, 400 new students pay $2,500 tuition ($1,000,000 gross revenue), served by 120 full-time staff (our assumption).

Institution (Official Sources)
Origins in 1998 as Alif Creative Academy; owned by Nusantara Education Group since 2017; University College status in 2021.
Motto: “A Leader For All”. Address: Lot 13, Persiaran Kewajipan, USJ 1, 47600 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.
68 MQA-accredited programmes across 7 Schools and 4 Centres.
Scale & Revenue
400 new students a year × $2,500 first-year tuition = $1,000,000 gross annual tuition revenue.
120 full-time staff (stated as an assumption for this project).
Customers: Malaysian school leavers, working adults and international students.
