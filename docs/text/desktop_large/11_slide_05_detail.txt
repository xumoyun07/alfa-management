Goal & Financial / Marketing Objectives
Overall Goal: transform ALFA into a paperless university with 65% direct social media enrolment.
SMART 1 (Financial): 260 direct students × $375 avoided commission = $97,500 gross savings; net $55,500 after the $42,000 plan.
SMART 2 (Marketing): grow Instagram from 2.1K to 20K followers (5 Reels/week) and direct intake from 0% to 65% in 12 months.
Digital Objective
SMART 3 (Digital): launch ALFA OneHub (CRM+ERP+HRM) by Month 4; cut student admin wait from 5–7 days to under 24 hours and paper use by 90%.
Efficiency: cost per direct student = $42,000 ÷ 260 = $161.54; blended cost = $94,500 ÷ 400 = $236.25 per student (baseline $400.00).
